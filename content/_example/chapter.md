This folder shows the chapter format and is not built into the app. A real introduction says in two or
three short paragraphs what kind of game the opening leads to, what the side playing it is aiming for,
and what the other side is counting on.

A blank line starts a new paragraph. **Bold** and *italic* work, and so do lists.

## Why 3.Nxe5 and not 3.Nc3
moves: 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nf6 3. Nxe5
arrows: Gf3e5 Rf6e4
squares: Ye4

A section explains one idea, plan or structure. The optional `moves:` line gives the position of its
diagram, and `arrows:` and `squares:` mark it up. Put these lines directly under the heading.

- Say what the idea is and when it works.
- Say what stops it, and what to do then.
